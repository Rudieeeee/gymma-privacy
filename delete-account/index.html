Delete GYMMA Account

Last updated: 4 October 2026

GYMMA (com.gymma.app) is published by Rudrh Kapoor.

Before publication

The in-app deletion feature has been implemented but its live Supabase deployment and verification are still required. This website is not yet published and does not automatically delete accounts. The publisher must monitor the support inbox and process verified requests.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email gymma.support@gmail.com from the email address registered to your GYMMA account.
  2. Use the subject GYMMA account deletion. Say that you want your GYMMA account and associated data deleted. You may include your GYMMA username to help locate the account.
  3. We will verify that you control the account before processing the request. If you cannot access the registered email, explain this; support must establish ownership before acting.

Never send your password, purchase token, bank details or payment-card details. This is a manually reviewed request route, not an automated web deletion form. No fixed response or deletion deadline is promised here.

Delete from the app

After the backend is deployed, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. Read the permanent-deletion warning, enter your current password, type DELETE, and select Permanently delete my account. Finish any active workout or purchase first.

The server must confirm deletion before local data is cleared. If verification or cleanup is interrupted, reconnect and use the retry option. Contact support if you cannot resolve it.

What is deleted

Your Supabase sign-in account, profile, cloud fitness/workout/gameplay save, Gold balance, Inventory and character progression, gym leaderboard entries, challenge completion records, active verified purchase records and Battle Pass entitlements are deleted. On the device performing successful in-app deletion, account-owned saves, caches, recovery backups, exercise notes/reference links and pending leaderboard submissions are cleared, and the app signs out.

Email requests do not remotely wipe another device. Clear GYMMA app storage on devices you control to remove remaining accessible local copies. Language/unit preferences and advertising consent choices are not account records. Provider logs, Android backups and infrastructure backups are governed separately; instant erasure of those copies is not claimed.

What is retained and why

Only the purchase platform, a SHA-256 fingerprint of a settled transaction identifier and the deletion timestamp remain in GYMMA’s purchase replay guards. No account ID, email, product ID, full transaction identifier or purchase token is kept in those guards. This limited security record prevents an already-credited purchase being credited again after deleting and recreating an account. The guards currently have no automatic expiry because expiry would reopen the replay risk.

Deletion does not refund or cancel Google Play purchases, erase Google’s own records, or transfer old Gold or Battle Pass rights to a new account. Requests received by email also create correspondence processed by Gmail; support should minimize and review its retention.

Privacy and contact

For partial data-deletion requests or privacy questions, email gymma.support@gmail.com. There is no separate automated partial-deletion feature.

Read the GYMMA Privacy Policy for the complete processing and retention information.
