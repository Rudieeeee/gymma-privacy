1. Information we collect

The information processed depends on the features you use:

  • Support correspondence: if you email us, your email address, message and information needed to verify and process your request are handled through Gmail. Do not include passwords or payment credentials.
  • Account information: email address, password submitted for authentication, username, account identifier, session credentials and account/profile timestamps.
  • Profile and preferences: age, height, body weight, units, training goals, training preferences and schedule, available equipment, language and other settings.
  • Fitness and nutrition information: selected activities, routines and plans; recorded sets, repetitions, loads, duration, distance and effort where supported; workout history, records and mastery; estimated calories, volume and fatigue; body-weight history and recovery check-ins such as sleep, soreness, readiness and energy; dietary goals, preferences, allergies, disliked foods, generated plans and custom meals.
  • Game and customization information: XP, levels and game attributes, quests, achievements, boss and Battle Pass progress, inventory, cosmetics, avatar appearance, Coach settings and generated Coach message history, gym visits and challenge results.
  • Location information: coordinates and accuracy supplied by Android when you grant location permission, nearby-gym search areas and records associated with gyms you visit or challenge.
  • Advertising and purchase information: consent choices and SDK/device information used by Google; purchased products, purchase tokens, transaction identifiers and entitlements.

Free-text names, notes and custom meal information you enter may contain personal information. Avoid entering information that is not needed for the feature.

2. Information stored locally

GYMMA uses on-device app storage for gameplay and fitness records, preferences, account-specific saves and recovery/conflict backups. Supabase session credentials are also persisted on your device so that a session can be restored. Passwords are submitted to Supabase for authentication; GYMMA does not deliberately save your password in its game records.

Nearby-gym search caches include the search center and discovered locations. Exercise notes/reference links, gym image/prestige caches, pending leaderboard submissions, cloud-sync metadata and device-wide language/unit preferences are also local. Google’s SDKs may store their own consent or advertising state.

“Stored locally” does not mean “never uploaded”: the account cloud-save feature synchronizes the records described next. Local account caches and sync backups are not automatically deleted when you log out.

3. Account and cloud data

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud storage for the existing GYMMA project. Signing up, signing in, confirming an email, recovering a password and refreshing a session communicate with Supabase. Authentication emails are sent through the project’s configured email-delivery service.

While signed in, the app automatically synchronizes durable profile, fitness and gameplay saves when connected, including workout history and active workouts, routines, records, mastery, avatar and Coach customization, quests, inventory, Battle Pass and boss state, Diet information, recovery/analytics records, equipment settings and gym progress. Offline changes can be uploaded on reconnect; Sync now also requests synchronization.

Passwords and Supabase session tokens are not included in the ordinary cloud-save payload. Local Gold balances and the applied-reward ledger are excluded from that payload, while verified purchase/economy records are handled separately on the server.

Your account identifier and username are readable by other authenticated users under the current profile access rules. Email and the private fitness cloud-save are not part of that shared profile. Gym leaderboards can show your username, account identifier, rank, Gym Points and completed-challenge count. Signed-in challenge completions submit the gym identifier, challenge identifier and date; this can reveal that you participated at a particular gym.

4. Fitness and body-related information

GYMMA uses your profile, workout, nutrition and recovery information to create plans, adapt exercise selection, calculate estimates, show progress and generate game/Coach responses. Accessibility preferences can include difficulty standing, walking or running, limited upper/lower-body use, back/knee/shoulder sensitivity, low-impact or seated preferences. These fields and dietary allergies can reveal health-related information and may be included in synchronized plans and saves.

Avatar settings such as visual sex, body shape, muscularity, body fat, facial features and hair describe your chosen character. They are not verified medical or physical measurements. The current app does not provide a camera/photo-upload or microphone-recording feature. Coach and planning calculations run in the app; no external generative-AI service was found in these flows.

5. Location data

With your permission, GYMMA uses foreground location to place you on the World map, search for nearby gyms and check physical proximity when starting or finishing a gym challenge. The map listens for position updates while the map screen is focused and stops that listener on leaving it. Challenge checks may request higher accuracy. The current code does not request background location or record a continuous running GPS route.

Nearby-gym searches send your latitude, longitude and search radius to external OpenStreetMap Overpass servers. The default servers are overpass.private.coffee, overpass-api.de and maps.mail.ru; fallback searches can use more than one server. These operators also receive connection information such as your IP address.

OpenFreeMap supplies map styles and tiles. Tile requests disclose the viewed map area and your IP address, even though the app does not explicitly send your account or workout data with them. Selected gym images can be requested from Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata using the relevant gym/image reference. Gym participation is also stored in saves and leaderboard submissions. The proximity sample used at workout completion is checked locally rather than submitted as raw GPS evidence by that flow.

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings. Nearby positioning and proximity-gated challenges may then be unavailable. No precise coordinates or fitness profile are supplied by the GYMMA advertising adapter for ad targeting.

6. Advertising and consent

GYMMA integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for banners and interstitial ads. Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) refreshes consent information and displays any required consent form before the app initializes advertising and requests ads. Where UMP requires privacy options, you can reopen them from GYMMA Settings. Consent outcomes can prevent ad requests; non-personalized ads are not the same as an absence of data processing.

The app requests non-personalized ads and sets a G-rated maximum ad-content setting. The app’s ad gate requires a loaded profile with a declared age of at least 18; unknown or younger ages do not enable advertising. Users aged 13–17 may use GYMMA, but GYMMA does not request ads for them in this release. Unknown or missing ages do not receive advertising. Adult users remain subject to the existing Google UMP/privacy consent flow.

Google’s advertising SDK can process IP addresses (including inferred approximate location), advertising/app/device or account-related identifiers where available, ad/app interactions, and performance or diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google and applicable advertising partners can process this information under their own terms and the consent choices presented. GYMMA does not attach your email, username, workout history, body measurements or precise location to its ad requests.

EEA, UK and Switzerland users may see consent/privacy messaging depending on Google’s region determination and the publisher’s configured messages. The AdMob European consent/CMP message still requires dashboard publication; it is not claimed to be live or verified. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Google advertising privacy information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

7. Purchases

Google Play Billing handles purchases of Gold and seasonal Battle Pass access. Google processes payment information through Google Play; GYMMA’s purchase code does not collect your card details. The app sends its account identifier to Google Play in the purchase account-association field.

For verification, the app sends a product identifier and purchase token to a Supabase verification function with its authenticated session. That function verifies the purchase with Google’s Android Publisher service and associates successful transactions with your account. Server records include account, product and transaction identifiers, verification/purchase timestamps, Gold settlement and Battle Pass entitlements. Restore/reconciliation reads verified records. If the external purchase service is unavailable, verification may fail; Google Play purchase configuration and a live purchase test still require completion; successful real-money verification or replay testing is not claimed.

8. How we use and share information

We process information to operate accounts and cloud restoration, track your activities, personalize plans and Coach messages, calculate game progression, support World/gym interactions and leaderboards, deliver permitted advertising, verify purchases and prevent duplicate or unauthorized rewards.

Information is transmitted to the service providers described here for those features. Account/profile and leaderboard visibility are described in section 3. The app does not expose private workout cloud records as leaderboard data. Opening an external social or informational link lets the destination process your browser/network information under its own policy.

No separate developer behavioral-analytics or crash-reporting SDK was found in the current app. The app’s “Analytics” screens calculate fitness summaries; their saved input records can synchronize to Supabase. This does not prevent Google’s SDKs or service hosting infrastructure from processing usage, diagnostic or request logs.

10. Data retention

The current app does not implement a general timed expiry for account, fitness, cloud or purchase records. Saves and account-specific local backups can remain while stored on your device or in the account’s cloud records. Logging out caches account data for future sign-in; it is not deletion. Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not delete remote records. Android-managed backup copies may also exist depending on device settings.

Gym image/search caches have refresh/reuse rules, which are not a guaranteed deletion schedule for provider logs or all saved data. Provider retention is governed separately. When account deletion is completed, active account-linked records are deleted as described below. We retain only the purchase platform, a SHA-256 fingerprint of each settled transaction identifier, and the deletion timestamp to prevent the same purchase being credited after account recreation. These guards contain no account ID, email, product ID or full purchase token. Fingerprints are pseudonymous security records, not a claim of anonymous data. They currently have no automatic expiry because removing them would reopen purchase replay. Provider logs, backups and records held by Google are governed separately; no unsupported deletion deadline is promised.

11. Security

The app uses authenticated access for cloud and purchase operations, account-specific local caches, and Supabase row-level ownership rules for private cloud records. Trusted purchase settlement is restricted to privileged server code; the mobile app uses a publishable Supabase key. Network services in the reviewed configuration use HTTPS.

These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. We do not claim end-to-end encryption or a particular encryption-at-rest scheme for local saves, session storage or cloud data. Protect your device and account credentials.

Clearing Android app storage removes accessible app-local saves and sessions but does not erase your account or cloud data.

Depending on applicable law, including laws in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent for consent-based processing, and complain to your data-protection authority. Withdrawal does not retrospectively invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

The publisher must confirm the applicable legal grounds for account/fitness processing, any additional condition for health-related data, and international-transfer arrangements. Accepting a privacy policy or an advertising consent form alone is not a claim that all health-data processing has a valid legal basis. You may submit privacy and deletion requests to gymma.support@gmail.com.

13. Age and intended audience

GYMMA is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 13 should not create or use a GYMMA account. The profile form rejects newly entered ages below 13. Users aged 13–17 may use GYMMA without advertising in the current release.

Advertising is enabled only for users with a loaded profile and a declared age of at least 18, subject to consent and the app’s other advertising safeguards. Unknown or missing ages and malformed or legacy profiles below 13 do not enable advertising. An invalid legacy profile must be corrected through profile setup; it is not silently treated as an adult. Age checks rely on user-provided information and are not verified age assurance or parental consent.

The intended Google Play target-age groups are 13–15, 16–17 and 18 and over. Google Play target audience, Data Safety and store declarations still require completion; no under-13 group is intended.

14. Account and data deletion

GYMMA now includes Settings → Account → Delete Account. It requires your current password, typing DELETE, and a final permanent-deletion action. Deletion removes the Supabase Auth account, profile, cloud save, account economy balance, gym leaderboard entries and challenge completions, active verified transaction records and Battle Pass entitlements. The account’s accessible local fitness/gameplay saves, account cache, sync/recovery backups, exercise notes/reference links and pending leaderboard submissions on the requesting device are removed only after server confirmation, and the app signs out. Device language/unit preferences and Google consent settings remain. Other devices and Android/provider backups are not remotely wiped by this action.

Deployment status: the production SQL migration and delete-account Edge Function are deployed. A physical Android test confirmed deletion, rejection of the deleted credentials and removal of the Supabase account/profile. Purchase replay protection is installed; no real-money purchase replay test has been performed. A failed or uncertain server request does not cause local data to be erased; the app offers verification/cleanup retry. An unresolved request or expired session may require support assistance.

For a request without access to the app, see Delete GYMMA Account, or email gymma.support@gmail.com from the address registered to your GYMMA account. We must verify control of the account before deletion. Do not send your password, purchase token or payment-card details. The website does not delete accounts automatically.

A minimal purchase fingerprint is retained for replay prevention as described under retention. Deleted Gold balances, progression and Battle Pass rights cannot be recovered by recreating an account or replaying an old settled purchase. Account deletion does not refund, cancel or erase purchases or records held independently by Google Play. Contact Google Play for purchase/refund matters.

Signing out, uninstalling or clearing app storage alone does not delete the remote account. If you want particular data removed without deleting your account, contact us; no separate automated partial-deletion system is claimed.
